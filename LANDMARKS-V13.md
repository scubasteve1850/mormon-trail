# Landmark destinations

Nine stops now open an illustrated arrival and a saved destination hub. Players can explore, rest, repair, trade, and prepare for the next stretch. Garden Grove offers planting for those who follow; Mount Pisgah offers a mending day; Winter Quarters offers the condensed spring preparation chapter; South Pass can set careful footing. Shared-camp opportunities appear at Sugar Creek and Platte River Camp. Stop activities and discovery rewards cannot be collected twice. Purchase/rest/repair days use current daily rations.

Road previews use distance, difficulty, transport, pace, selected approach, vehicle wear, and current food consumption to estimate days and food before weather or delays. Family moments use living travelers, current needs, and recent memories. Named arrival scenes avoid depicting deceased family members as present.

Exploration collects one of nine discovery cards, with a short historical note and a National Park Service source. The collection appears at the top of Story in an expandable section. Discoveries award two morale once and 75 points in the arrival score. No history quiz is required. Unresolved arrival/hub/discovery/trade phases resume after reload.

### Trading-post prompt
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: background art for a polished family-friendly Mormon pioneer adventure game, wide landscape 1536x1024. Create a beautiful cinematic painterly 3D-animated-game-style illustration of a modest 1847 frontier trading post in the broad prairie near the Rocky Mountain trail corridor, inspired by Fort Bridger. Low rough-hewn log buildings, a modest rough timber fence, covered emigrant wagons with wooden spoked wheels and iron rims, a few distant travelers in 1840s clothing, supply barrels and sacks. Warm late-afternoon sunlight, rich grass, airy blue sky and distant low ridges. Grounded, welcoming adventure, highly detailed materials, believable scale. Establishment buildings and wagons occupy upper-middle of image; clear darker foreground for interface overlay. Illustrative historical fiction rather than a claimed architectural reconstruction. No modern objects, no rubber tires, no large military stone fort, no signage, no text, no borders, no logos.

### Independence Rock prompt
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: wide landscape 1536x1024 arrival illustration for a polished Mormon pioneer adventure game. Cinematic painterly 3D animated game environment, detailed believable materials and soft golden morning light. Independence Rock in Wyoming: a broad, low, smoothly rounded elongated granite dome rising out of open sagebrush country beside a small winding river. Make the landform a wide smooth granite mass, not a sharp mountain or desert red mesa. A very small covered pioneer wagon and distant travelers in 1840s clothing show the enormous scale of the rounded rock. Warm pale granite, olive sage, airy blue sky with soft clouds, far distant hazy uplands, inviting sense of discovery. The landmark occupies the upper-middle of the composition, darker grasses and trail in the lower foreground allow interface text overlay. Illustrative historical fiction, not an exact site reconstruction. No readable inscriptions, no modern roads, no modern structures, no rubber tires, no typography, no logo, no border.

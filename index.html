About this map

North is up. Geographic locations, river courses, and political claims are generalized; this is not a surveyed trail. The Platte camp is fictional. Iowa became a state in December 1846; Utah and Wyoming did not yet exist as states. Indigenous homelands crossed the political lines shown.

# Character and playability review

Family travelers now have heads about one seventh of their height, shaped shoulders and waists, separate trousers and boots, fitted sleeves, and distinct dresses and aprons for Eliza and Sarah. The existing portraits and roster are retained. Two-bone knees and elbows preserve limb lengths, with opposing arm motion, planted-foot steps, gentle weight shifts, and distinct step phases. The same figures appear beside the wagon, at camp, and in river scenes.

Trail guidance prioritizes urgent food, an unwell traveler, vehicle repairs, upcoming supply needs, and low morale. This helps younger players connect decisions to the family’s condition without adding another screen. Existing travel, history chapters, difficulty settings, resource management, map, music, and save behavior are retained.

Validation: browser inspection of the animated scene and phone Trail view; existing river, save, supply, and full reference-journey tests pass on all difficulties. Added checks cover fixed leg lengths through 121 gait poses and actionable Trail guidance. These remain illustrated 2D figures; native iPhone animation performance has not been measured.
